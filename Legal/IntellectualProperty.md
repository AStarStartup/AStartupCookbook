### Trademarks

This section describes US terminology using USPTO guidance. It does not determine anyone's ownership, registration status, enforceability, or remedies. Ask `attorney` about those conclusions and about use in other jurisdictions.

#### Common-law rights and registration

The USPTO distinguishes owning a trademark through use from having a federal registration. It says registration is not required and provides broader rights and protections than an unregistered mark.[1] This is not a distinction between a "property right" and a mere "usage right". Do not infer a specific company's rights from its name, domain, or use of a symbol.

#### TM, SM, and ®

USPTO guidance permits TM for goods and SM for services even without an application.[1] The registration symbol ® is for a federally registered mark and only for the goods or services listed in that registration.[1] An application is not a registration. TM or SM is a claim of trademark rights, not proof of registration or a substitute for clearance.

The author reports in [issue #107](https://github.com/AStarStartup/AStartupCookbook/issues/107) that a grant panel challenged his use of TM even though he considered it appropriate. That is the author's account, not evidence that the mark was registered. The earlier text reversed this anecdote and invented a registration status.

#### Agent procedure

1. Read the owner's approved brand guidance. Preserve existing approved usage, including Astartup™ where specified; do not upgrade it to ®.
2. For a requested registration claim, obtain the registration record, owner, jurisdiction, goods/services, and a verification date. Route uncertainty to the owner and `attorney`.
3. Keep an approved-use table in the project's brand records: mark, display form, evidence, scope, reviewer, and date. Do not populate it with guessed registrations.
4. Ask before changing published symbols or giving legal assurances. Consistent punctuation does not establish legal rights.

There is no verified registration table for AStartup, Kabuki Starship, AStarship, or their products in this chapter. No universal fee, filing timeline, enforcement outcome, or instruction to register every brand is asserted here.
