This section covers the different types of licensing forms, and royalties.

Licensing is how a creator or a company grants the right to use something it owns, in exchange for a payment or a condition, and the two questions to answer first are what is being licensed and what the licensee may and may not do. The common forms are a royalty on each unit or dollar, a fixed fee, a share of revenue, or a cross-license of rights. Route the specific contract, the tax treatment, and any regulatory condition to the appropriate specialist; this page is a stub until it names the working decision for which form fits which situation and what the founder should never sign without review.
