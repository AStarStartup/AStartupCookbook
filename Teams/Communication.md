Communication is the process of getting a decision, a fact, or a request from one person to another without it changing, and of keeping the record so the next person can pick it up. The working rule for a small team is to put the decision and the reason in writing, in the place the team already looks, and to keep the conversation separate from the record. The failure mode is the decision that lives only in a person's head or in a channel that scrolls away, because the next person cannot find it. This page is a stub until it names the specific channel and record the team uses for decisions.
