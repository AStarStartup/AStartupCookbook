A team structure is the answer to who is responsible for what, and who the person with the question asks. The working rule for a small team is to keep the structure shallow and explicit: every person knows their own scope, the person they report to, and the person who is responsible for the thing they are blocked on. The failure mode is the structure that is implied rather than stated, because the implied structure is the one that changes without anyone noticing and the work falls through the gap. This page is a stub until it names the specific structure the team runs and the responsibility each person holds.
