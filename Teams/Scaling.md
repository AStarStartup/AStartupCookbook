This section will be about how to scale up teams in different time frames, short, medium, and long-term.

Scaling a team is the process of adding people and the structure that lets the work continue to happen, in short, medium, and long time frames. The working rule: the structure must exist before the people who need it arrive, because the structure that is added after the team is too big for it is the one that costs the most to add. The short time frame is the next hire and the process that hire needs; the medium is the team that reports to a person who is not the founder; the long is the organization that can run without the founder in every decision. This page is a stub until it carries the specific structure for each time frame.
