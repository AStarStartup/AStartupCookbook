Team etiquette is the set of small, repeated behaviors that keep a team working without the founder having to referee every exchange: how a request is made, how a decision is recorded, how a mistake is raised, and how a disagreement ends. The working rule is to make the etiquette explicit and written, because the etiquette that is only understood is the etiquette that is violated the moment the team is stressed. This page is a stub until it names the specific etiquette the team runs.
