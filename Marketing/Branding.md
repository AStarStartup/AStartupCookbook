Branding is the set of things a person remembers about you when they are not in front of you: the name, the look, the tone, and the one thing you are known for. It is built before the launch, not after, because the first impression is the one that is cheapest to get right. The minimum working brand is a name, a one-line description of who it is for and what it does, a consistent look across the places it appears, and a tone that would be recognizable out of context. This page is a stub until it carries a worked brand brief for a specific product.
