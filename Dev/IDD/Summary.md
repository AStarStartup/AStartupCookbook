Agentic Driven Development (ADD) is the working method for this chapter: a mission ticket defines the problem, the solution, the files it touches, and one section per sub-commit, and the commit messages track those sections letter for letter. The pages in this directory cover the mission and parking tickets, the change-management procedure, and the session record that ties work to the ticket. Until the individual pages are finished, treat this summary as a draft.
