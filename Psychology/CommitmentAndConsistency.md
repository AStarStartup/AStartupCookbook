People will go to great lengths to remain consistent with what they previously said they view as commitments. The working version for a founder is to get the small, reversible commitment first, because the person who has agreed to the small thing is the person who will agree to the next step to stay consistent with themselves. The caution is the same one that applies to every influence technique in this chapter: the consistency is real, and it can be used to push someone toward a decision that is not in their interest, so the founder owes the customer the information they need to make the commitment freely.
