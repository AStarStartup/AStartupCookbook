Classical conditioning is the process by which a response that was originally tied to one stimulus becomes tied to a different stimulus that is repeatedly paired with it. In product and marketing, the working version is the response a customer learns to a cue that reliably predicts the thing they wanted. The caution is that the learned response is only as strong as the pairing, and it fades without it, so a cue that is not consistently followed by the thing it promises will stop working. This page is a stub until it carries a worked example of a product cue and the response it should train.
