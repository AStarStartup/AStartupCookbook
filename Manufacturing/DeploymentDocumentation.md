Deployment documentation is the record that lets someone who was not in the room put the product into service: what is required, in what order, how to confirm each step worked, and what to do when a step fails. It is written for the person doing the deployment under pressure, not for the person who designed it. This page is a stub until it carries a worked deployment procedure with the checks and the failure paths named.
