Manufacturing documents are the set of records that define what is made and how it is made: the parts list, the bill of materials, the process steps, the tolerances, and the acceptance test. They exist so that the product can be made the same way more than once, by someone other than the person who first made it. This page is a stub until it names the minimum document set a first product needs before it leaves the prototype stage.
