title: "Mental Floss"
---

Mental floss is the short, regular practice of clearing the working mind so it can hold the next problem. The standing version is a brief, bounded task with no stakes: a walk, a short physical task, or a few minutes of writing the open items down so they are not being carried in the head. It is not a treatment and it is not a guarantee; it is a reset that works for some people and not others, and the founder should test whether it helps rather than assume it does.
