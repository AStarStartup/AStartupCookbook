Energy management is the self-care version of the entrepreneurship page by the same name: the founder's own energy is the resource the company is built on, and it is spent, not created. The practical set is the same as the work page, applied to the person: the sleep, the movement, the breaks, and the hours the best work happens. This page is a draft because the routine is personal and will be different for every founder.
