A white paper is a position paper: it takes a claim about a problem and argues a specific solution, with the evidence and the limits of that evidence stated on the page. It is not a product brochure. The working structure is a problem statement, the evidence for it, the proposed solution, the alternatives rejected and why, and the conditions under which the paper would be wrong. This page is a stub until it carries a worked example of that structure.
