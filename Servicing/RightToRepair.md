Servicing includes maintenance, repair, and the interaction with customers and with the people and owners in the service sector. The right-to-repair question is whether the customer, or a third party the customer chooses, is allowed to fix the product, and what the founder owes them in documentation and parts to make that possible. The working position is that the founder who wants the customer to keep the product should make repair possible and documented, because the customer who can fix the product is the customer who keeps it.
