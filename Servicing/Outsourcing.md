Outsourcing is the decision to hand a piece of the work to someone else, and the test is whether the work is something the founder should be doing at all. The working rule: outsource the work that is commodity, well-scoped, and not the core of the product, and keep the work that is the product or the thing only the founder can do. The failure mode is outsourcing the core because it is convenient, and keeping the commodity because it is uncomfortable. This page is a stub until it names the specific decision for which work a first team should outsource and which it should keep.
