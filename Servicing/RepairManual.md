So what exactly is going to happen when your product breaks?

The repair manual is the record that tells a person who was not in the room what to do when the product breaks: the symptom, the likely cause, the fix, and how to confirm it worked. It is written for the person doing the repair under pressure, and it is the thing the customer checks when the product fails. The standing question is what actually happens when the product breaks, and the answer is the manual. This page is a stub until it carries a worked repair procedure with the symptoms and the failure paths named.
