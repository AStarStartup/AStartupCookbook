This is important because every entrepreneur needs to know what type of engineer you’re going to need to hire.

Every entrepreneur needs to know which kinds of engineer the work actually requires, because hiring the wrong kind is the most expensive mistake a first team can make. The useful distinctions are by the work, not by the title:

* Software: who builds the product and who keeps it running.
* Hardware: who makes the physical part and who certifies it.
* Data: who turns raw records into a decision you can act on.
* Infrastructure: who makes the systems repeatable and safe to scale.

For a solo or first team, the first hire is usually the person who removes the bottleneck you are currently working on, not the person with the most impressive title. State the bottleneck, the output expected, and the constraints before you describe the role. This page is a stub until it names the specific roles a solo founder should know how to brief.
