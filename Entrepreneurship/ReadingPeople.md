Reading people is forming a working model of what a specific person will do next, from their stated interests, their past behavior, and their incentives, and then acting on that model in a way that can be corrected. It is not mind-reading and it is not a fixed list of tells; it is a record of what you predicted and how it turned out. The useful output is a short note per person: what they said they want, what they have done when it cost them something, and what that implies for the next conversation. This page is a stub until it carries a worked example of that record.
