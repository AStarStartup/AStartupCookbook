Money management for a startup is keeping the cash that keeps the lights on separate from the cash that funds growth, and knowing exactly how long the first kind lasts. The minimum working set: a runway number in months, a fixed monthly burn, and the date the cash runs out if nothing else changes. Route the tax, securities, and contract conclusions to the appropriate specialist; this page is a stub until it carries the working cash view and the decision rule for when to cut, when to raise, and when to ship.
