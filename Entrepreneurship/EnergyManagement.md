Energy management is scheduling the work to the energy you actually have, not the other way around. The practical version: find the hours when your best work happens, protect them for the decision-heavy tasks, and put the routine work in the hours you would otherwise waste. It is not a claim that everyone peaks in the morning; the founder's own peak is the one that matters, and it should be measured, not assumed. This page is a stub until it carries a specific schedule template the founder can run.
