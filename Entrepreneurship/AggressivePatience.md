Aggressive patience is moving quickly on the things that move the needle and refusing to rush the things that need to be right. The test is whether a step is reversible and cheap: if it is, do it now and learn; if it is expensive to reverse, spend the time to get it right before you commit. The failure mode is the opposite: rushing a structural decision and stalling on the cheap, reversible ones that would have taught you something. This page is a stub until it carries a worked example of a decision sorted by reversibility and cost.
