A beginner's mindset is the habit of not assuming you already know the answer, and of asking the question a newcomer would ask. It is the discipline that stops an expert from answering the question they think was asked instead of the one that was actually asked. The marshmallow challenge is the standing example: the teams that plan and test before they build outperform the teams that start building immediately.
