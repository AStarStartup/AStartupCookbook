### Market Validation

Market validation is accumulating evidence for a specific business decision. It is not a badge earned by a survey, a demo, or a model-generated market estimate.

Use [Problem Solving](../Engineering/ProblemSolving.md) as the canonical analysis procedure. It connects the problem, baseline, buyer, alternatives, economics, and next experiment. Use [Customer Interviews](../Analytics/CustomerInterviews.md) to collect accounts of actual behavior and current workarounds.

Keep these claims separate:

* The problem occurs for a defined customer in a defined situation.
* A proposed solution improves the relevant outcome.
* A buyer with authority will adopt and pay for the offer.
* The improvement persists through repeat use.
* You can reach and serve those buyers with acceptable cash and founder-hour costs.

Choose an experiment and stop rule for the weakest consequential claim. Record eligible prospects, recruitment, nonresponses, actual payments or usage, timing, and limitations. A small paid pilot can justify another test; it does not establish a market-size forecast or scalable demand.

Agents may organize and challenge the evidence. They must not simulate interviews and count them as validation, send outreach without permission, or treat a hypothesis as a requirement. A valid analysis can conclude that the founder should not build.
